Mid-Level Backend Software Engineer

Responsibilities:
- Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services and RESTful APIs.
- Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code.
- Optimize application performance and troubleshoot production issues.
- Design database schemas and write efficient SQL queries.
- Collaborate with frontend engineers, QA, product managers, and DevOps teams.
- Participate in code reviews and architectural discussions.
- Contribute to CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation.
- Monitor applications and improve reliability and observability.

Required Skills:
- 3–5 years of backend development experience.
- Strong proficiency in Java, Python, Go, or Node.js.
- Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases.
- Knowledge of distributed systems, caching, and messaging systems.
- Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP).
- Understanding of Git, testing frameworks, and CI/CD.

Preferred:
- Experience with microservices.
- Exposure to monitoring tools such as Prometheus or Grafana.
- Knowledge of system design and performance tuning.
